The **Don Muerte Social Club** manifest is not open yet. Signup fields appear on this site, but submitting one records nothing — no address is sent anywhere, stored, or retained.

## What we collect

- Nothing from the signup fields. Submissions are discarded in your browser and never transmitted.
- No placeholder addresses held behind the scenes.

## What we will not do

- Pretend an address was enrolled when no list exists.
- Sell or share signup information if enrollment opens later.
- Begin collecting without updating this notice first to name the provider and the real handling practice.

## What you can always do

- Submit nothing. The fields are inert, and leaving them empty costs you nothing.
- Return to this page when the manifest opens to review the updated terms before joining.
- Read the **Disclaimer** to confirm what this brand is and isn't selling.

Before the manifest opens, this policy must be updated with the connected provider, retention practice, unsubscribe path, and deletion process. Updates will be dated and visible at the top of the page.
