Somewhere past last call, beyond the agave fields and the cemetery gate, there's a cantina that only opens after midnight. The bell rings once. The dead start dancing. Don Muerte pours. Legends get made.

That cantina is **La Cantina del Último Trago** — *the cantina of the last drink* — and RIP Tequila is the bottle it always pours first.

The cantina runs on eight characters and five bottles. Don Muerte hosts. Cantina Jack pours. Campana Roja rings the bell. La Rosa Negra closes the night. Padre Proof blesses the parts that need blessing. El Sepulturero handles the rest. Los Maracas keep the music alive. El Vigía watches the desert road in case anybody else is on the way.

The bottles — Blanco, Reposado, Añejo, Extra Añejo, Midnight Agave Reserve — each belong to one of them. Each one has its own toast. Each one shows up at its own hour of the night.

What you take home is the bottle on the table after everyone else has gone. What stays behind is the cantina.

> *"No actual spirits. All the spirit."*
