Most luxury tequila brands tell you about their great-grandfather’s copper pot still, volcanic spring water filtered through ancient jade, and jimadores who sing to the agave under a harvest moon. 

We don't have a great-grandfather. We have **Don Muerte**. He didn't inherit a distillery; he won the late shift in a coin toss against the sunrise, and he's been running **La Cantina del Último Trago** (*The Cantina of the Last Drink*) ever since.

Nestled exactly three miles past last call, right where the desert highway turns into a rumor, the cantina operates on a simple philosophy: **Rest In Proof.**

### The Eight Who Run the Room

A legendary tequila requires legendary witnesses. The cantina doesn't run on corporate boards or celebrity endorsements — it runs on eight permanent residents who take their craft with deadly seriousness:

- **Don Muerte** hosts the floor with an unreadable grin and a single shot glass he never empties.
- **Cantina Jack** mans the mahogany bar, pouring crisp Blanco while filing your deepest secrets under *unnecessary details*.
- **Campana Roja** watches the brass bell above the door. When she rings it, your evening officially transitions from a casual drink into permanent folklore.
- **La Rosa Negra** takes the stage when midnight hits, singing boleros so haunting they’ll make a grown man apologize to his ex from 2014.
- **Padre Proof** sits in the corner booth, offering official ecclesiastical absolution for your worst cocktail napkin ideas.
- **El Sepulturero** walks the back lot with a shovel and a leather manifest, respectfully burying lost arguments and abandoned dignity.
- **Los Maracas** provide the unstoppable skeletal rhythm section, ensuring nobody sits down until their boots give out.
- **El Vigía** stands sentinel on the desert road, nodding silently as late arrivals navigate their way toward the glowing neon sign.

### Five Bottles. Five Hours of the Night.

Our five expressions — **Blanco, Reposado, Añejo, Extra Añejo, and Midnight Agave Reserve** — aren't just aged in oak. They're aged in atmosphere. Each bottle marks a distinct chapter of the evening, from the bright optimism of the opening round to the heavy, solemn clarity of the 3:00 AM confessional.

You can't buy these bottles in stores (our distribution network exists entirely on the astral plane). But you can collect the evidence, wear the marks, and toast like you mean it.

> *"No actual spirits. All the spirit."*
