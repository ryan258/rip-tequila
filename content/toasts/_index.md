**Don Muerte** gives one toast a night. Everyone else is welcome to write theirs on a napkin and hand it to **Padre Proof**, who blesses each one, files it in the booth, and occasionally reads the best of them aloud at dawn to the candle.

This is the collection. Borrow freely. Toasts are the only thing in the cantina that grow more valuable when repeated.

## Toasts of the House

> *"Life's short. Toast accordingly."*
> — Don Muerte, every night since the coin toss

> *"To the ones who aren't here, and the ones who are only here because they couldn't find the exit."*
> — Don Muerte, on a busy Saturday

> *"The bell rang. You're in. Don't make me regret it."*
> — Campana Roja, very quietly

> *"I pour. You explain tomorrow."*
> — Cantina Jack, who has never once asked for the explanation

> *"I bless this mess. Every part of it. Especially that part."*
> — Padre Proof, gesturing at the dance floor

> *"Here lies your judgment. It had a good run."*
> — El Sepulturero, lowering a glass and his voice

> *"Shake now. Explain later. Or never. Never is also fine."*
> — Los Maracas, in unison, mid-song

> *"Every heartbreak deserves a chorus. Some deserve two."*
> — La Rosa Negra, before the encore she never gives

> *"You made it. Most don't take the road this far."*
> — El Vigía, the only thing he has ever said, according to one guest

## For Every Occasion

### Birthdays

> *"Another year closer to the good tables. Cheers."*

> *"May you live long enough to become a story somebody tells wrong."*

### Weddings

> *"To love, which outlasts everything, including the open bar."*

> *"May your arguments be short, your dances be long, and your bell ring only once."*

> *"Till death do you part — and then, frankly, you'll probably still end up at the same cantina."*

### Breakups

> *"To the one who got away. Keep going. The road is long and El Vigía is watching."*

> *"May they hear a bolero about you someday and not know why they're crying."*

### Retirement

> *"To permanent time off. You're halfway to being one of us."*

### Mondays

> *"It's not the end. It only feels like it. We'd know."*

### Funerals

> *"See you at the bar. Save me the seat by the plant."*

> *"Go easy on the bartender. He already knows everything."*

### When You Arrive Late

> *"I was just passing through."*
> — Four hundred thousand guests, collectively

### When You're the Only One Not Drinking

> *"To the witness. Somebody has to remember this correctly."*

## How to Give a Proper Toast

Don Muerte has never explained his method. The regulars have worked it out by watching.

1. **Wait for quiet.** Don't ask for it. Just raise the glass and wait. It will come.
2. **Toast someone who isn't the loudest person in the room.** It's almost never the right person.
3. **Name someone absent.** Every good toast has an empty chair in it.
4. **Keep it short.** If it needs a second paragraph, give it to Padre Proof instead.
5. **Raise the glass to shoulder height.** Higher looks like a question. Lower looks like a secret.
6. **Don't drain it.** The toast is the point. The glass is just the witness.

> *"The best toast you'll ever give is the one somebody quotes back to you after you're gone."* — Padre Proof
