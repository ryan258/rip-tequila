**RIP Tequila is a fictional parody brand created for storytelling, design, merch, and entertainment. No actual spirits are sold here. If you drink, drink responsibly. Hydrate. Get a safe ride. Do not operate a vehicle, forklift, horse, hearse, or emotional conversation after drinking.**

Everything on this site — the bottles, the characters, the cantina, the membership card, the slogans, every certification or seal that appears on a label — is a creative concept. None of it represents a real spirits product, a real distiller, a real distributor, or a real point of sale. Where copy mentions tasting notes, batch numbers, or production, those references are part of the parody, not literal claims.

### What this means

- **No real spirits** are produced, bottled, distributed, or sold under the RIP Tequila name.
- **No real certifications, seals, or government approvals** are referenced or implied.
- **No real distillery, region, or aging process** is being described.
- **Real merch may exist.** Coasters, apparel, stickers, hats, and the membership card are real and shippable. Bottles are not.

### If you drink

If you choose to drink an actual tequila — produced by an actual distillery, sold by an actual retailer — please drink it responsibly. Hydrate. Get a safe ride home. Don't drive. Don't operate machinery. Don't start an emotional conversation you weren't planning to finish. Make it a night worth haunting, not regretting.
