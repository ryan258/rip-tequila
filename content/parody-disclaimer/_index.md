**RIP Tequila is a fictional parody brand created for storytelling, world-building, high-concept design, and limited merchandise. No actual spirits are distilled, bottled, or sold here. If you consume real alcohol in the real world, drink responsibly. Hydrate. Book a safe ride. Do not operate a motor vehicle, forklift, mechanical bull, hearse, or delicate romantic negotiation while under the influence.**

Everything showcased across this site — the five bottle expressions, the tasting notes, the eight cantina characters, the seals, the batch numbers, and the awards from fictitious afterlife tasting panels — is an elaborate satirical art project. None of it represents a commercial spirits product, an active distillery, or a licensed retail entity.

## The Plain-English Breakdown

- **Zero Physical Alcohol**: No liquid tequila is manufactured, aged, or distributed under the RIP Tequila name.
- **Zero Real Certifications**: All seals, medals, and regulatory stamps are artistic parody.
- **Genuine Merchandise**: Our apparel, barware coasters, low-profile hats, and vinyl sticker sheets are 100% physical, high-quality, shippable products.

## If You Toast in the Real World

If you choose to enjoy genuine tequila crafted by a licensed distillery, treat it with respect. Hydrate between glasses. Protect your friends. Never drive under the influence. And remember: the best nights are the ones you can actually recall the next afternoon.
