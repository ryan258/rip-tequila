Every serious spirit has a wall of medals. Ours hangs in the back office, where **El Sepulturero** keeps it dusted, catalogued, and arranged in order of how hard each one was to believe.

What follows is the complete public record. Judges' names have been withheld at the request of the judges, most of whom requested it from beyond.

The 1811 double-double has been audited three times. Each audit concluded that the judges tasted the Blanco, awarded it gold, forgot, tasted it again, and awarded it gold with greater confidence. The committee has since added a rule about this. The rule is named after us.

## Special Recognitions

### Most Improved by Aging, Posthumous Division

Awarded to **RIP Extra Añejo**, which spent thirty-six months in deep charred oak and emerged, in the words of the citation, *"more mature than any of the judges."*

### Excellence in Silence

Awarded to **Don Muerte** for a toast in which he raised his glass, said nothing for four minutes, and lowered it to a standing ovation. The committee described it as *"the most articulate silence in competition history."* He did not attend the ceremony. He sent the glass.

### Outstanding Contribution to Rhythm, Unrestrained

Awarded to **Los Maracas** by the Regional Association of Floorboards, which also filed a complaint. Both documents hang side by side.

### Least Requested Encore

Awarded to **La Rosa Negra**, with distinction. Nobody requests an encore because nobody has ever survived the first set with enough composure to form the words.

### Lifetime Ban, Honorary

Awarded to the cantina by the Highway Patrol of a state that no longer appears on maps. The letter says *"in recognition of persistent disappearance."* El Vigía considers it the house's finest honor.

## A Note on Scoring

All house expressions are evaluated by the cantina's independent tasting panel: **Padre Proof** and his candle. The panel has never scored an expression below 97 points. Critics occasionally question the panel's independence. Padre Proof points out that the candle has voted against him twice and is still lit.

> *"The medals are real. The competitions are a matter of perspective."* — El Sepulturero
