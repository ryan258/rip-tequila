El Sepulturero handles the night after the night. When the toasts are done and the bell has rung itself out and most of the cantina is on its way to becoming somebody else's story, he's the one walking the back lot with a long shovel and a small notebook he calls *"the manifest."*

Bad decisions get buried respectfully in his line of work. Lost arguments get filed. The names never leave the book. He's not threatening — he's organized. The shovel is a tool. The judgment is the joke, and he's the only one in the cantina who doesn't laugh at it.

He doesn't say much. The notebook says it for him.

> *"Leave the judgment. Take the story."*
