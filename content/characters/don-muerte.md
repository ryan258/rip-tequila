Don Muerte runs the room. He's the one who decides who walks through the door, which toast actually lands, and when the night turns into a story worth telling again. He's been hosting La Cantina del Último Trago for as long as anybody has been arriving — and arriving is the only verb that matters at the cantina.

He wears a sombrero. The skull beneath it is up for interpretation; he won't confirm it, and the regulars stopped asking. He carries one shot glass, which he pours from and never drinks. He keeps a single agave plant behind the bar. The plant is older than most of the legends in the room.

When he speaks, it's usually to make a toast. Listen for it. He only gives it once a night.

> *"Life's short. Toast accordingly."*

## What he pours

**RIP Añejo.** Dark, considered, gold at the edges. The bottle he reaches for when the night actually deserves it.

> *"Proof You Lived."*
