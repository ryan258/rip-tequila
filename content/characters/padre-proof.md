Padre Proof handles the rituals nobody else wants to officiate. He blesses bad ideas. He witnesses minor vows. He presides over toasts that probably should have been edited before they were delivered. His paperwork is impeccable. His judgment is, by design, generous.

He keeps a small black book that the regulars assume is liturgical. It mostly contains drink notes, names, and one running list titled *"still owed."* He never reads from it in public. He doesn't need to. The book exists, and the existence is the blessing.

If you're not sure whether the round you're about to pour is a good idea, find him. He'll bless it either way.

> *"Blessed are the almost responsible."*

### What he pours

**Padre Proof Ceremonial Añejo**, a special edition for vows that probably shouldn't be vows. He recommends one shot, which he reframes as a sacrament.

> *"I bless this mess."*
>
> *"Your sins are between you, the lime, and the receipt."*
