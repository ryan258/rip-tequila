**Los Maracas** are the unstoppable rhythmic powerhouse of the cantina. Two skeletal maestros with one shared mission: make sure the cantina never falls into somber decorum.

Where Don Muerte handles ceremony and La Rosa Negra handles melodrama, Los Maracas handle pure, unadulterated joy. Their instruments include a vintage trumpet that hasn't cooled down since 1952, a Spanish guitar missing half its strings yet somehow sounding immaculate, and a pair of hand-carved gourd maracas that drive the entire room to its feet.

They do not take song requests, they do not understand the concept of an intermission, and they communicate entirely through infectious syncopated tempo. If you find yourself dancing on a bench at 1:30 AM with a stranger in a bolo tie, Los Maracas are 100% responsible.

> *"The dead have better music."*

## What they pour

**Los Maracas Blanco Infusion**, the high-energy party pack edition. Uncapped, served cold, and consumed while moving.
