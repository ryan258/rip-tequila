Five bottles. One family. The whole lineup lives in the cantina, but each pour belongs to a different character and a different hour of the night.

**Blanco** comes off the shelf first. Cantina Jack pours it without looking up. Clean, bright, no wood — the kind of bottle you came in for if you came in for anything at all.

**Reposado** is the house pour. Smooth, warm, on the table by the time Campana Roja stops ringing the bell. The bottle the night gets named after.

**Añejo** is Don Muerte's. Dark, considered, gold at the edges. The pour that gets a hush. He gives the toast once a night, and it always belongs to this bottle.

**Extra Añejo** is the long-aged one. El Sepulturero handles the rounds that go this deep. You don't celebrate this bottle. You witness it.

**Midnight Agave Reserve** is for the late arrivals. El Vigía watches the road. If a Reserve is on your table, you took the long way in.

> *"Born in Agave. Buried in Legend."*
