Welcome to the tasting flight that defies standard municipal zoning laws and basic common sense. 

Each expression in the **RIP Tequila** collection is crafted from 100% fictional Blue Weber Agave, harvested by jimadores who refuse to answer direct questions, and bottled in heavy flint glass designed to double as a conversational centerpiece or an impromptu paperweight for your legal disclaimers.

### The Five Sacred Pours

- **RIP Blanco**: The opening act. Cantina Jack slides it across the bar before you even finish clearing your throat. Crisp, unaged, and entirely unburdened by wood or second thoughts.
- **RIP Reposado**: The house staple. Rested just long enough in charred oak to develop a smooth, seductive amber glow. The glass Campana Roja pours the second the brass bell chimes.
- **RIP Añejo**: Don Muerte's personal benchmark. Aged 18 months in toasted casks until it develops the gravitas of an ancient secret. The pour that demands everyone at the table lower their voices.
- **RIP Extra Añejo**: El Sepulturero’s dark reserve. Three long years asleep in the back vaults. A heavy, smoky pour for deep conversations, old grievances, and nights that require a shovel.
- **Midnight Agave Reserve**: The midnight wanderer’s pour. Guarded by El Vigía on the desert perimeter. For those who arrived well after the road stopped making sense.

> *"Born in Agave. Buried in Legend. Remember to hydrate."*
