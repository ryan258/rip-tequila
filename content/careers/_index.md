La Cantina del Último Trago has kept the same eight on staff for longer than most countries have kept the same flag. Turnover is zero. Retention is total. Every exit interview on record was conducted by the employee's own descendants.

But the room keeps getting fuller, and even legends need a hand. We're looking for passionate, detail-oriented individuals who thrive after dark and don't mind a long commute.

## Why Work Here?

- **Permanent Time Off.** Unlimited, effective immediately upon arrival, and impossible to roll back.
- **Flexible Hours.** Every hour is the late hour. You'll never be asked to work a morning, because there aren't any.
- **Family Atmosphere.** Literally. Several of the staff are your ancestors, and they have opinions about your posture.
- **Free Meals.** Pan de muerto, nightly, from the ofrenda. Leave some for the guests.
- **Career Growth.** Join as an apprentice. Become folklore. There is no step after folklore. That's the point.
- **Wellness Program.** Los Maracas run a mandatory dance class every night at the second chorus. Attendance is not optional and has never been low.
- **Retirement Plan.** Already handled.

## Open Positions

### Assistant to the Caretaker

**Department:** Back Office · **Reports to:** El Sepulturero · **Shift:** Closing until whatever comes after closing

Help maintain the Manifest, the dignity plot, and the chain of custody for every argument interred on the property.

**You will:**

- Dig a respectful number of shallow holes each night, labeled.
- Issue receipts for buried items, in triplicate, with a witness.
- Assist with intake of abandoned excuses from the fencepost.
- Never, under any circumstances, dig something back up because a guest "really needs it."

**You have:**

- Strong shoulders and a stronger filing system.
- The ability to keep a straight face while burying someone's karaoke rendition.
- Your own spade (preferred). The house spade is not shared.

### Junior Mourner

**Department:** The Crimson Stage · **Reports to:** La Rosa Negra · **Shift:** Midnight set only

Provide quiet, dignified weeping during ballads, so first-time guests understand it's allowed.

**You will:**

- Sit at the front table and feel things, visibly but tastefully.
- Pass handkerchiefs down the row during the second verse.
- Hold the candle steady when the stage door opens.

**You have:**

- At least one unresolved heartbreak, ideally from before 2014.
- Professional-grade lower lip control.
- No song requests. If you've ever made a request, please do not apply.

### Bell Polisher, Brass

**Department:** Ceremony · **Reports to:** Campana Roja · **Shift:** Daylight, which you will spend alone

Keep the bell bright without ringing it. This is harder than it sounds and has ended careers.

**You will:**

- Polish the bell daily with a soft cloth and steady breathing.
- Inspect the red ribbon for fraying, wind damage, and fingerprints from guests who thought they were funny.
- Report any accidental chime to Campana Roja immediately and in person.

**You have:**

- The steadiest hands you know.
- Comfort working in total silence for long periods.
- No interest whatsoever in finding out what happens if it rings early.

### Fencepost Attendant

**Department:** The Road · **Reports to:** El Vigía · **Shift:** Dusk to arrival

Support the outer boundary by watching headlights and saying nothing at all.

**You will:**

- Stand near the fencepost with a hat on.
- Tip the hat, or not, according to criteria you will never be told.
- Keep the vulture company. The vulture does not need company. Do it anyway.

**You have:**

- Exceptional patience and an outstanding hat.
- Fluency in nodding.
- Your own boots, broken in, desert-rated.

### Barback, Bottom Shelf

**Department:** The Long Mahogany Bar · **Reports to:** Cantina Jack · **Shift:** All of it

Keep the bar stocked, the limes cut, and the most vulnerable bottles as close to the floor as possible during the second chorus.

**You will:**

- Cut limes. So many limes.
- Restock glassware before Jack reaches for it, which is before he decides to.
- Catch at least one falling bottle per night. Two if the trumpet is warm.

**You have:**

- Fast hands, quiet feet, and a memory you don't talk about.
- The discipline to never, ever tell a guest what they ordered last October.

### Absolution Clerk

**Department:** The Booth · **Reports to:** Padre Proof · **Shift:** After 11:00 PM, when it gets busy

File confessions, reconcile the ledger, and keep the candle trimmed for panel duty.

**You will:**

- Sort blessed napkins by sin, then by vintage.
- Reconcile *Still Owed* against Jack's tab and record the disagreement in the official book of disagreements.
- Serve as alternate juror if the candle is ever unavailable. It has never been unavailable.

**You have:**

- Infinite tolerance and very good handwriting.
- No judgment, professionally speaking.

### Tempo Consultant

**Department:** The Moonlit Patio · **Reports to:** Los Maracas · **Shift:** Until the boots give out

Join the rhythm section as a third set of hands. There have been two sets for a very long time, and they would like to argue with someone new.

**You have:**

- Your own percussion, or the willingness to become percussion.
- No concept of an intermission.

## Positions Not Currently Open

- **Host.** Taken. Don Muerte won it in a coin toss against the sunrise, and the sunrise has never asked for a rematch.
- **Head Bartender.** Taken. Please stop leaving résumés behind the bar. Jack reads every one, nods, and uses it as a coaster.
- **Lead Vocalist.** Do not ask. She will hear you.

## How to Apply

There is no form. Write your name and your best excuse on a slip of paper and leave it at the fencepost on the desert road. El Vigía will collect it, El Sepulturero will file it, and if the hat tips, someone will be in touch.

Hiring timelines are flexible. The current average is about one hundred and twelve years. We think that's a reasonable amount of time to get to know each other.

> *"Nobody quits the cantina. They just get promoted to legend."* — Cantina Jack

*La Cantina del Último Trago is an equal opportunity employer. We do not discriminate on the basis of age, era, century, state of corporeality, or how you arrived.*
