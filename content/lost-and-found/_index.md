## Long-Term Holdings

Some items have been in the Manifest so long they've become part of the building.

- **Four hundred thousand (400,000) excuses**, beginning with *"I was just passing through."* Stored in the excuse vault, sorted by plausibility. The bottom drawer is reserved for *"This is my last one,"* and it is the fullest drawer in the vault.
- **The original plans for a quiet evening**, submitted in 1923 and never collected. The ink has faded. The optimism has not.
- **Three premium bottles** from Jack's top shelf, rattled loose by a Los Maracas downbeat. They are not lost. They are resting at floor level. Do not call them lost in front of Jack.
- **A suitor's apology, undelivered**, left at the stage door decades ago with a single black rose. The rose was claimed. The apology remains on file. La Rosa Negra visits it once a year and does not open it.
- **The sunrise's coin**, from the night Don Muerte won the late shift. The sunrise has never asked for it back. El Sepulturero keeps it polished just in case.

## Claims Policy

Items may be claimed in person by presenting:

1. A valid receipt from El Sepulturero, with matching provenance number.
2. One witness who was present at the time of loss and remembers it the same way you do.
3. A sincere explanation of why you want it back.

To date, no claim has satisfied requirement two. Nobody ever remembers it the same way.

## Unclaimed Property

Items unclaimed after one hundred years are entered into the permanent collection. Exceptional pieces may be displayed on the back office wall, beside the medals. Particularly embarrassing pieces are buried with full honors at the edge of the dignity plot, beneath a small wooden marker that reads only: *It seemed like a good idea.*

There are a lot of those markers. The plot has been expanded twice.

> *"Nothing is truly lost here. Some things are just finally where they belong."* — El Sepulturero
