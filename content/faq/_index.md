faqs:
  - q: "Is RIP Tequila an actual alcohol company?"
    icon: "skull"
    open: true
    a: |
      **No.** RIP Tequila is the house label of La Cantina del Último Trago — the afterlife cantina that opens only after last call, where the staff is deceased and the five bottles pour exclusively on the astral plane. **No actual spirits are distilled, bottled, or sold here.**

      The bottles have never left the cantina. Neither has the merch. Neither, for that matter, has most of the staff.

  - q: "What is the point of a tequila that nobody can buy?"
    icon: "agave"
    a: |
      The same point as every great tequila: the toast. The bottle was always just the excuse.

      Most houses spend a fortune telling you about a great-grandfather, a copper still, and water filtered through something volcanic. We have a host who won the late shift in a coin toss against the sunrise. We think that's a better story, and we didn't have to bottle anything to tell it.

  - q: "Is the cantina open to the public?"
    icon: "door"
    a: |
      It is open to anyone who can find it, which narrows the public considerably. There is no address, no sign on the highway, and no listing anywhere you'd think to look. Most guests arrive by running out of reasonable excuses to go home. See [Find Us](/find-us/) for the closest thing we have to directions.

  - q: "Do I need a reservation?"
    icon: "book"
    a: |
      No. **El Vigía** already knows you're coming. He knew before you did. If his hat tips when your headlights pass the fencepost, **Campana Roja** will have one hand on the cord by the time you reach the door.

  - q: "What's the dress code?"
    icon: "hat"
    a: |
      Come as you are, or as you were. Hats are welcome. Boots are encouraged, since **Los Maracas** do not let anyone sit down during the second chorus. Sunglasses after midnight will be confiscated by Don Muerte, who will look at them for a long time before putting them in the [Lost & Found](/lost-and-found/).

  - q: "Can I request a song?"
    icon: "microphone"
    a: |
      No. **La Rosa Negra** has not taken a request in living memory, or the other kind. If you shout a song title, she will sing a different song, slower, about someone who shouted. The reconstructed setlist is on [The Jukebox](/jukebox/).

  - q: "What happens when the bell rings?"
    icon: "bell"
    a: |
      It doesn't mean closing time. It means you're in. The ring marks the exact moment you stop being a visitor at a bar and become part of the cantina's permanent folklore. Nobody has ever been un-rung.

  - q: "What does \"Rest In Proof\" mean?"
    icon: "monument"
    a: |
      It's the house motto, and the only one Don Muerte has ever approved without changing a word. Companion lines include *"Proof You Lived"*, *"The Bell Rang. You're In."*, *"Here Lies Your Judgment"*, and *"No Actual Spirits. All the Spirit."*

  - q: "Why does my bottle look empty?"
    icon: "bottle"
    a: |
      Because it is. Every RIP Tequila bottle is completely empty, which **Cantina Jack** considers the greatest packaging innovation since the cork. Nothing to spill, nothing to break, and nothing to explain tomorrow. Our [Commitments](/our-commitments/) page covers the environmental benefits in more detail than strictly necessary.

  - q: "Can I actually buy the merchandise?"
    icon: "box-open"
    a: |
      **Not yet.** Every piece on the [Merch](/merch/) page is still in the back office, being catalogued by El Sepulturero, who refuses to release anything until he has filed it twice. There is no checkout, no cart, and no price that anyone will actually charge you. When something can genuinely ship, the site will say so plainly and show you exactly where to buy it.

  - q: "Where can I find a physical bottle to display on my bar?"
    icon: "glass"
    a: |
      You can't purchase physical bottles at retail. If you see a bottle of RIP Tequila in the wild, it is either a prop, a handmade replica, or a temporal anomaly. If it is a temporal anomaly, please do not drink it. Back away slowly and let El Vigía know.

  - q: "How much does it cost to join the Don Muerte Social Club?"
    icon: "card"
    a: |
      **Nothing.** No dues, no subscription, no hidden tiers. The roster isn't open yet — El Sepulturero has not found a pen that writes on the ledger — so the [Social Club page](/don-muerte-social-club/) shows the charter without asking for your email. Nobody's email is being collected. Not yours, not anyone's.

  - q: "Is the cantina haunted?"
    icon: "eye"
    a: |
      No. It's staffed.

  - q: "Can I have my wedding here?"
    icon: "rose"
    a: |
      Many have tried. The ceremony is lovely, the toast is unforgettable, and the bell rings exactly once. The reception tends to run long. The longest one on record is still going. The [Book of Last Words](/toasts/) has a few wedding toasts if you need one.

  - q: "Is anyone hiring?"
    icon: "pencils"
    a: |
      Always, and forever. Openings include Assistant to the Caretaker, Junior Mourner, and Bell Polisher. Benefits include Permanent Time Off. See [Careers](/careers/) and leave your application at the fencepost.

  - q: "I think I left something at the cantina."
    icon: "shovel"
    a: |
      You did. Everybody does. It has already been tagged, photographed by candlelight, and entered into El Sepulturero's Manifest. Review the public extract on the [Lost & Found](/lost-and-found/) page. Claims require a witness who remembers the night the same way you do. Nobody ever does.

  - q: "What if I drink actual tequila in the real world?"
    icon: "warning"
    a: |
      Be of legal drinking age where you are. Drink responsibly. Hydrate aggressively. Order a cab or rideshare. Do not operate cars, forklifts, horses, hearses, or delicate emotional relationships under the influence. Make it a night worth haunting, not regretting. The full guidance is on [Responsible Language](/responsible-language/).
