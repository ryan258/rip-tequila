The Origin Story

Some Legends Never Die.
They Just Open A Cantina.

Most luxury tequila brands tell you about their great-grandfather’s copper pot still, volcanic spring water filtered through ancient jade, and jimadores who sing to the agave under a harvest moon.

We don’t have a great-grandfather. We have Don Muerte. He didn’t inherit a distillery; he won the late shift in a coin toss against the sunrise, and he’s been running La Cantina del Último Trago (The Cantina of the Last Drink) ever since.

Nestled exactly three miles past last call, right where the desert highway turns into a rumor, the cantina operates on a simple philosophy: Rest In Proof.
