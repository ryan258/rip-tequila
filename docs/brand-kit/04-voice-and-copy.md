# 04 — Voice and Copy

## The joke

Premium spirits marketing already sounds unhinged — master-blender mythology, small-batch mystique, three-generations-of-craft claims, celebrity-tequila gold rush. RIP Tequila says the quiet part out loud, from a cantina full of dead staff who take it exactly as seriously as a real brand would.

**On target:** fake tasting notes, mock founder/legacy claims, fake awards/seals, parody of how seriously premium brands sell themselves.
**Off target:** spooky/skull for its own sake with no wink at spirits marketing underneath.

## Voice

Dry, deadpan, premium-first. Short lines. One joke per surface. Humor in the details, never in the headline. Never encores the binge.

- Headline: straight-faced premium claim (“Rest In Proof.”)
- Details: absurd footnote (“Aged in regret. Finished in lime.”)

## Taglines (locked rotation)

| Line | Use |
|------|-----|
| Rest In Proof | Master tagline — everything |
| Proof You Lived | Añejo hero, glassware, coasters |
| Born in Agave. Buried in Legend. | Front labels, box sides |
| The Bell Rang. You’re In. | Reposado/Campana Roja, launches |
| Here Lies Your Judgment. | Extra Añejo, patches, regret humor |
| No Actual Spirits. All the Spirit. | Disclaimers, footer, 404 |
| Save Water, Drink Tequila! | Matchbooks, party, slogans |

Retired (never ship): “Rest in Pour”, “Bury Me in Lime” as a headline, “One Shot Closer to the Afterparty”.

## Tasting-note formula

Real cue + absurd emotional cue + brand-world detail.
Example: “Oak, vanilla, roasted agave, and the confidence to make one more questionable toast.”

Reuse: roasted agave, citrus, vanilla, oak, pepper, smoke, dark chocolate, coffee, caramel, dried fruit, desert air, gentle spice, bad decisions, soft regret, midnight confidence, distant maracas, better stories, mild forgiveness.

Fake details: Batch Midnight No. 13 · Approved by Don Muerte · Poured by Cantina Jack · Blessed by Padre Proof · Witnessed by El Vigía · Buried by El Sepulturero · Announced by Campana Roja · Sung by La Rosa Negra.

Production lines: “Distilled in the highlands of imagination.” · “Bottled somewhere past midnight.” · “Made for fictional legends and real stories.”

## Responsible parody (mandatory)

Every packaging, merch, and party surface carries one of:

- Fictional parody brand. Drink responsibly. Haunt responsibly.
- No actual spirits. All the spirit.
- For parody, merch, and storytelling purposes.
- Do not operate a vehicle, forklift, horse, hearse, or emotional conversation after drinking.

Never: real ABV %, proof numbers, “100% agave” claims, fake government seals, certification marks, binge encouragement, false production claims. Compliance check: `npm run check:claims` must print nothing (it also runs in CI).
